# Motion references and implementation

## Material actually reviewed

The user supplied three inline visual references and an MP4 (720 × 1280,
30 fps, approximately 16 seconds). The MP4 was inspected as a contact sheet.
It is the download associated with Pinterest pin `649855421266314822`.
The uploaded media is not copied into the public repository.

- White typographic ribbons wrapping a black sphere: separate cylindrical
  bands, alternating type weights and directions, a tilted global axis and
  black spherical core. Implemented as **Type rings** with six editable bands.
- Black text distorted over a red ball: curved UV mapping, organic radial
  deformation, specular lighting. Implemented as **Liquid type** with editable
  latitude strips and media textures.
- White condensed type on an inward-curving black sheet: a narrow waist,
  widening edges and scrolling text. Implemented as **Type tunnel**.
- The MP4 alternates oversized italic words, rotating glyphs, circular letter
  arrangements and scrolling repeated text on white, black, yellow, orange
  and violet fields. Implemented as **Letter ballet** and **Pattern roll**.

These are adaptable interpretations of the supplied motion language, not
frame-identical reproductions. Typeface, palette, wording, content, camera,
shape deformation, repetitions and timing can be changed in the editor.

## Additional original variations

**Ribbon helix** extends the ribbon idea into a continuous spiral with independent
editable segments. **Type textile** maps text or uploaded content onto an animated,
tessellated sheet with coherent waves and lighting. Both are labelled LAB /
«Авторская вариация» in the library and are not claimed to be found online.
